Privacy Policy

This policy explains how June Alliswell Calendar accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data.

Private, non-public service

June Alliswell Calendar is operated exclusively for its owner's private personal project (Alliswell). The application itself is available only on the owner's local environment and non-public Tailscale network, and is not offered to external users. Only this informational website is public.

June Alliswell Calendar는 소유자 개인의 프라이빗 프로젝트(Alliswell) 전용 서비스입니다. 애플리케이션은 소유자의 로컬 환경 및 외부에 공개되지 않는 테일스케일(Tailscale) 내부 네트워크에서만 이용되며 외부 사용자에게 제공되지 않습니다. 공개된 것은 이 안내 웹사이트뿐입니다.

1. Google data the application accesses

The application requests Google Calendar scopes (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly and/or https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events). It accesses calendar entries in the owner's Google Calendar account, including event summaries, dates, times, and descriptions needed for personal schedule management in the Alliswell system.

2. How Google data is used

Google user data is used solely to view and synchronize personal schedules and events for the owner within Alliswell. The application does not use Calendar data for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to personal schedule management.

3. Data storage and protection

Synchronized calendar records and OAuth credentials are stored strictly on the owner's private, self-managed local machine and accessed securely via Tailscale. The service is never exposed to the public internet. Access is limited solely to the application owner, with appropriate system safeguards in place to protect against unauthorized access.

4. Data sharing and transfer

Google user data is not sold, rented, disclosed, or transferred to third parties. No Google user data is used to serve advertisements or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only for as long as the owner uses the application for personal schedule coordination. The owner can delete stored records and OAuth tokens from the local machine at any time. Google access can also be revoked via Google Account security settings at any time, immediately ending the application's ability to access Google Calendar.

6. Limited Use disclosure

June Alliswell Calendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the application's handling of Google user data changes. The effective date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.

8. Contact

Privacy and support questions may be sent to the developer contact address shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
